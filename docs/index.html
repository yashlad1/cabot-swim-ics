Cabot Open Swim

Open swim at the Barletta Natatorium, Northeastern Boston, as a subscribable calendar feed. Rebuilt from the university schedule every 3 hours.

Subscribe in Calendar

Or paste this URL into your calendar app:
https://yashlad1.github.io/cabot-swim-ics/cabot-swim.ics

On macOS set Location: iCloud, not On My Mac, or it won't reach your phone. Subscribe on one device only.

Schedules move for meets, lessons, lifeguard shortages and maintenance. Treat this as a prompt to swim, not proof the pool is open. Source
